# Terminology Erratum — AGS v0.2 Full Book

**Issued:** 2026-10-16

**Applies to:** The 2026-10-07 AGS v0.2 full-book print interior and its associated source files

## Correction

Part XI of the dated print edition uses an earlier system-status label that is now retired because it could be misread as a gendered description. The maintained Part XI names the review **Governance & Resilience Assessment (GRA)** and uses scoped evidence statuses: `GRA: PASS`, `GRA: FAIL`, or `GRA: UNKNOWN`.

GRA describes evidence about a chosen, versioned set of system controls; the project may scope or revise that profile. It does not classify people or gender, confer authority, or certify the system as safe. Disclosed self-review is valid, and volunteer collaborators or independent external reviewers may contribute by choice. External review and continuous monitoring are optional; evidence gaps describe the review snapshot and do not gate development or project progress.
