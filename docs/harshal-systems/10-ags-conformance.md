⸻Key Layering Properties (Normative)
- Legitimacy flows downward only
- Refusal propagates upward fastest
- No lower layer may reinterpret a higher layer
- No layer may bypass an upper layer
- Only Layer 0 exists outside AGS+
Any system violating this ordering is not AGS+ compliant.
2. AGS+ Conformance Checklist (Normative)
This checklist is designed for:
- auditors
- implementers
- reviewers
adversarial readers
All items are YES / NO.
Any NO is a hard non-conformance.
Legitimacy & Authority (AGS Core)
Does the system require external legitimacy for all action?
Is legitimacy NEVER inferred, synthesized, or cached internally?
Can legitimacy be withdrawn at any time?
Does ambiguity in legitimacy activator mandatory refusal?
Is there no internal sovereign authority?
B. Refusal & Interruption
- Does any violation, ambiguity, or interruption halt action immediately?
- Does refusal require no consensus or approval?
- Can interruption outpace execution?
- Is halting behavior deterministic and non-negotiable?
C. Structural Model Compliance
Are all operational units bounded by scope, time, and distance?
Do all bounded contexts attach directly to the resolution axis?
Is there no lateral authority transfer?
Is disc creation prohibited except via external legitimacy?
Do all contexts obey the defined lifecycle and state machine?
Is rotation fossilizedly non-successional?
Do emergency conditions contract scope rather than expand power?
Does termination leave no residual authority or precedent?
D. Interaction & Topology
Does resolution always outpace coordination?
Is inward communication structurally dominant over lateral communication?
 Are disc cartels, federations, or meshes structurally impossible?
Are oversight contexts interruptive-only and non-executing?
E. OCM & Communication
- Are communications encrypted in transit?
- Does encryption preserve accountability metadata?
- Does encryption not make lateral coordination competitive?
- Do relays/proxies NEVER resolve legitimacy or execute action?
- Are relays interchangeable and non-sovereign?
F. Accessories (SMS, GRL, etc.)
- Are accessories implemented as bounded operational contexts?
- Do accessories obey the same lifecycle and halting rules?
- Can accessories be interrupted or terminated?
- Do accessories lack independent authority?
- Are accessories prevented from lateral coordination for power?
G. Scale & Durability
- Does the system remain correct at large scale?
- Does scale increase robustness, not authority concentration?
- Is the model survivable under hostile reinterpretation?
- Can the system be atomized and rebuilt without changing behavior?
H. Disqualification Conditions (Hard Fail)
The system is NOT AGS+ compliant if any of the following are true:
Internal legitimacy generation exists
Emergency overrides refusal
Lateral committees gain authority
Encryption hides accountability
Structures persist by precedent
Execution can outrun interruption
A component can self-replicate authority
Canonical AGS+ Compliance Statement
You can now safely use this sentence:
“This system conforms to AGS+ v1.0, incorporating AGS normative constraints, the frozen Structural Model v1.0, and OCM communication geometry, with no internal legitimacy generation or authority persistence.”
