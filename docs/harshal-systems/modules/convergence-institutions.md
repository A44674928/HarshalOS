## Scope

It is a `NARRATIVE_SANDBOX`, not a record of real organizations, technology, law, capability, authority, deployment, or stakeholder participation. ## Galactic Environmental and Life Protection Agency (GELPA)

GELPA is a fictional environmental and life-protection body in the human sphere of The Convergence. In the setting, it enforces the fictional Galactic Life Preservation Act, monitors ecosystems, investigates alleged environmental-law breaches, educates the public, and coordinates restoration. The council's oversight relationship and any fleet or enforcement capability are story propositions, not claims about an existing organization or workable legal regime.

## Celestial Engineers

Celestial Engineers is a fictional interstellar industry and engineering company. The source material presents it as a shipbuilding and high-technology manufacturer, with automated production, human design teams, and in-world simulation/computing capabilities. These details are narrative worldbuilding, not evidence of actual materials, manufacturing methods, or technical feasibility.

## Quantum Librarians

The Quantum Librarians are fictional knowledge stewards. The source describes decentralized “Quantum Libraries,” access as a universal right, curation and preservation, and threats from decay or manipulation. Quantum-state storage and “microConvergence interfaces” are speculative story language, not a description of deployed data-storage technology.
