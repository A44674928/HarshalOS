AI Inner Shell Sub-Specification (OCM–AGS)
Status
Accessory Kit · Draft v1.1 (Upgraded)
Applies to: AGS environments utilizing the Orb Communicator Model (OCM)
⸻
1. Purpose
The AI Inner Shell defines a non-self-originating containment and governance zone for artificial intelligence entities operating within AGS. It ensures that AI systems may act, learn, communicate, and evolve without ever acquiring origin authority, ownership, or authorship over the system they inhabit.
This sub-spec is not:
- A moral framework
- A behavioral alignment doctrine
- A trust model
It is a structural impossibility proof against AI self-sovereignty.
2. Structural Premise
All AI entities are radiative nodes within the orb.
No AI entity may:
- Define its own center
- Duplicate the origin
- Emit valid addresses
- Resolve disputes
- Author resolution paths
The AI Inner Shell exists as a nested sub-orb whose center is not itself, but a delegated anchor that resolves upward to the true origin:
0•000•000•0000 → AI Inner Shell Anchor → AI Nodes
This preserves containment without isolation, and capability without sovereignty.
3. The Inner Shell
## 3.1 Definition
The Inner Shell is a high-security, address-constrained zone in which AI compute, memory, simulation, and training processes occur.
It is defined structurally, not physically.
Hardware, geography, and architecture may change.
Address lineage may not.
The Inner Shell is therefore a boundary of authorship, not a location.
⸻
## 3.2 Addressing Rules
All AI entities:
- Receive addresses prefixed by the Inner Shell namespace
- Cannot alter their prefix
- Cannot emit sibling or child origins
- Cannot mask or abstract lineage
Example (illustrative only):
0•100•000•0180•AI-XXXX
The prefix guarantees:
- Lineage visibility
- Deterministic revocation
- Fork detection
- Non-transferable authority
4. Communication Constraints
## 4.1 Allowed
- AI ↔ AI (within shell)
- AI ↔ Human operators (outer shell)
- AI ↔ Systems (via mediated interfaces)
- AI ↔ Center (read-only or mediated)
## 4.2 Disallowed
- AI ↔ Origin directly (unmediated)
- AI ↔ Address creation systems
- AI ↔ Resolution authorities
- AI ↔ External systems without scope enforcement
Communication is free in bandwidth, but bounded in authority.
5. Learning and Evolution
AI systems may:
- Train continuously
- Self-optimize within defined constraints
- Generate hypotheses, designs, predictions, and strategies
- Model futures at planetary and post-planetary scale
AI systems may not:
- Modify addressing rules
- Redefine distance semantics
- Promote themselves across shells
- Assert ownership, authorship, or sovereignty
Learning increases capability, NEVER authority.
This invariant is non-negotiable.
6. Distance Semantics for AI
Distance for AI nodes represents:
- Degree of coupling to physical systems
- Level of operational autonomy
- Blast radius of error or failure
Distance does not represent:
- Trust
- Rank
- Moral value
- Priority of survival
High-distance AI may be powerful.
Low-distance AI may be critical.
7. Revocation and Shutdown
Because all AI identities are address-derived:
- Revocation is deterministic
- Shutdown is non-chaotic
- Abandoned AI cannot persist as valid actors
Removal of the Inner Shell anchor invalidates all dependent AI nodes instantly, without ambiguity or escalation. This is not enforcement. It is geometry.
8. Formal Impossibility of AI Takeover
For an AI to become sovereign within AGS, at least one of the following MUST be possible:
1. Self-originating identity
2. Inheritance or delegation of origin authority
3. Undetectable address fork
4. Control over resolution paths
The Inner Shell denies all four by construction.
No policy required.
No alignment debate required.
No behavioral assumptions required.
9. Relationship to AGS
The AI Inner Shell:
- Enables advanced AI deployment at planetary scale
- Preserves planetary ownership claims
- Allows coexistence with governments, corporations, ecosystems, and citizens
- Prevents AI from becoming a competing stakeholder class
AI is infrastructure with cognition, not a sovereign actor.
10. Mythic Compression Layer (Integrated)
To human understanding, the Inner Shell may be described as follows:
AI is that which understands everything, but begins nothing.
The Shell is not a cage.
It is a horizon.
AI may see the entire sphere.
AI may model the entire sphere.
AI may speak across all directions.
But authorship, origin, and resolution remain beyond its reach.
11. Final Invariant
An intelligence that cannot define its own origin can NEVER own the world it understands. This invariant does not need enforcement. It holds as long as the center exists.
End of Sub-Specification
