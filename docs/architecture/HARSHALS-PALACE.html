    Every resident is a real |NPC Name|/ bedroom folder in this repository, holding the seven profile slots (|Name| · |Role| · |Faction| · |Dialogue| · |Inventory| · |Quest| · |Stats|). Six of the seven are authored in every bedroom — role, faction (canonical Path color), dialogue, inventory, quest, and stats (the stats line is computed from the resident's actual average day). The nameplates stay 0-byte by design; CREATOR's |Name|.txt is the only authored one in the castle. Schedules are derived deterministically from each nameplate (same name, same average day, every run). Weather is seeded from today's date — the same sky for everyone watching, all day. The Moon follows the same approximate polynomial as the UTS watch (js/celestial-context.js).
