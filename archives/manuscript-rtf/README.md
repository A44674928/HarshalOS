# Archived RTF chapter drafts

Rich Text Format working drafts of early Book II chapters. These are ARCHIVE
provenance — superseded snapshots kept for editorial history. The active
plaintext chapters published by the reader are under
[`manuscript/the-grand-river-foundation/`](../../manuscript/the-grand-river-foundation/);
later source-format drafts (Pages, RTF, DOCX) are under
[`Manuscript Working Drafts/`](../Manuscript%20Working%20Drafts/).

Files:

- `0 Point` — placeholder zero-point marker
- `Chapter 0.rtf` through `Chapter 3.rtf` — early RTF chapter drafts
- `Chapter 2.1.rtf`, `Chapter 2.2.rtf` — split variants of Chapter 2
