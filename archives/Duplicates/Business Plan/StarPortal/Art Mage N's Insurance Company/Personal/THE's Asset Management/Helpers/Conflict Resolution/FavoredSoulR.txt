Understanding the Conflict

    Divergent Goals: Each persona might have different priorities. For example, the Solar Champion might prioritize leadership and protection, while the Reaper might focus on enforcing harsh justice.

    Conflicting Methods: The methods used by each persona could clash. The Storm Bringer's aggressive approach might conflict with the Arbiter's desire for harmony.

    Emotional Tensions: The heir might experience emotional tensions as they try to balance the compassionate nature of the Lunar Sage with the ruthless efficiency of the Seer.
