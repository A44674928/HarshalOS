# Manuscript Working Drafts

Source-format working drafts for Book II — *The Grand River Foundation* and its
Convergence-universe companion material. These are preserved as author archive
material; they are **not** the active manuscript chapters served by the reader.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`Texts/Parts/`](Texts/Parts/) | Working drafts of the novel itself (Pages, RTF, DOCX) plus early UTS notes and placeholder `Test` / `Test alias` Finder aliases retained as-is |
| [`Texts/Story/`](Texts/Story/) | Worldbuilding / institutional one-pagers for Convergence-universe factions (GELPA, the Celestial Engineers, Quantum Librarians, GigaCorp, Hermes Intergalactic, Vulcan Freight, etc.) |

The active, reader-published chapters live in [`manuscript/the-grand-river-foundation/`](../../manuscript/the-grand-river-foundation/); see [`manuscript/README.md`](../../manuscript/README.md). The organized Convergence Institutions reference module for Book I lives under [`updates/2026-10-02/04-Institutional-Frameworks/Convergence-Institutions/`](../../updates/2026-10-02/04-Institutional-Frameworks/Convergence-Institutions/) and is documented in [`docs/harshal-systems/modules/convergence-institutions.md`](../../docs/harshal-systems/modules/convergence-institutions.md).
