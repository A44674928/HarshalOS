**Image treatment:** 640 × 360 pixel-art master enlarged 3× with nearest-neighbor scaling. Falling snow is a visual effect, not a forecast or a snow-load study.
**Status:** archival concept communication only; not survey, architectural, civil, MEP, clinical, legal, code, permitting, or construction documentation. The former cost-unlimited max-house is no longer the active baseline; the bermed wing remains an option to test in the lean plan.
**Workstream update (2026-10-17):** all concepts shown are assigned to **Engineered Connections** for digital coordination. This is a workflow label only; this historical film remains illustrative, with no engineered or approved connections. The current selected rate is $4,800 per rentable room-month for an all-in resident bundle, with shared occupancy not multiplying the room price; that selection is not market validation. The film's $4,800 and owner-share overlays are historical, not current labels. The user-provided county parcel screenshots display Unincorporated Coconino County, AR-5 (Agricultural Residential, 5-acre minimum), overlay N/A, Lighting Zone II, and “No permits found.” The displayed area-only screen passes by 0.59 acres (5.59 vs. 5.00); this is not overall zoning compliance. The screenshots have not been live-verified and the permit panel is not a certified history. Current ordinances, legal access, and survey inputs remain open, so code/zoning disposition is **NOT ASSESSED**; see `DIGITAL_CODE_ZONING_REVIEW.md` and `OUTPOST_ONE_UNIFIED_PLAN.md`. No local professional or agency review is represented.

## Interpretation and limits

- **Site orientation and geometry:** the site sequence begins with a view aimed generally toward the northeast, matching the user's video annotation as a visual cue only. The parcel outline is assessor/GIS geometry, not a boundary survey. The preliminary terrain surface is interpolated from ten EPQS spot samples; sample highs/lows are not verified site extrema. The site animation is a concept frame, not a surveyed placement plan. User-supplied observations about the apparent slope, fence, two-track, street, and road concern are not digitized as verified boundary or legal-access claims.
- **Building cutaways:** the side-on style keeps a simplified roof/facade edge, grade, and surrounding trees or exterior amenity in view while opening the near wall to reveal colored room bands. Labels are broad program zones for storytelling. They do not establish floor plans, dimensions, building footprints, code-compliant circulation, egress, accessibility, fire separation, clinical design, or constructability.
- **Flows and utilities:** moving dashes show conceptual relationships only. They do not establish actual routes, elevations/grades, pipe/cable sizes, demand, yield, pressure, temperature, electrical service, network throughput, storage, capacity, fire flow, treatment process, or approvals.
- **Tunnel:** the line is optional and dashed. Geotechnical conditions, slope/grade, drainage, structure, accessibility, fire/life-safety, egress, permits, cost, and benefit have not been assessed.
- **Business/program assumptions:** this archival film preserves an earlier $4,800/month bundle narrative and owner ≈1/12 capacity premise; the legacy workbook instead applies $4,800 per modeled bed. The owner has since selected $4,800 per rentable room-month as the current all-in bundle working price; the workbook denominator, owner-share premise, and film overlays are historical and do not define the current room schedule. The 20–50 kW sustained capability, 30+ day/no-STR direction, and 18% / 210 CY / five-ponderosa targets remain planning direction—not underwriting, metered capacity, takeoffs, or verified feasibility. The current lean-plan assumptions are in `OUTPOST_ONE_UNIFIED_PLAN.md`.
- **Medical and hospitality:** the emergency-capable medical concept is not a licensure or clinical-services determination. The spa is a wellness concept, not treatment. Greenhouse dining does not resolve food-service licensing, staffing, insurance, or cost.
- **No audio:** the feature is silent. Chapter titles and on-screen labels are the navigation and explanation layer.
