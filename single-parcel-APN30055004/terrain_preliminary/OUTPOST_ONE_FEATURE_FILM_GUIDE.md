## Interpretation and limits

- **Site orientation and geometry:** the site sequence begins with a view aimed generally toward the northeast, matching the user's video annotation as a visual cue only. The parcel outline is assessor/GIS geometry, not a boundary survey. The preliminary terrain surface is interpolated from ten EPQS spot samples; sample highs/lows are not verified site extrema. The site animation is a concept frame, not a surveyed placement plan. User-supplied observations about the apparent slope, fence, two-track, street, and road concern are not digitized as verified boundary or legal-access claims.
- **Building cutaways:** the side-on style keeps a simplified roof/facade edge, grade, and surrounding trees or exterior amenity in view while opening the near wall to reveal colored room bands. Labels are broad program zones for storytelling. They do not establish floor plans, dimensions, building footprints, code-compliant circulation, egress, accessibility, fire separation, clinical design, or constructability.
- **Flows and utilities:** moving dashes show conceptual relationships only. They do not establish actual routes, elevations/grades, pipe/cable sizes, demand, yield, pressure, temperature, electrical service, network throughput, storage, capacity, fire flow, treatment process, or approvals.
- **Tunnel:** the line is optional and dashed. Geotechnical conditions, slope/grade, drainage, structure, accessibility, fire/life-safety, egress, permits, cost, and benefit have not been assessed.
- **Business/program assumptions:** $4,800/month, owner ≈1/12 capacity, 20–50 kW sustained capability, 30+ day cohorts/no STR, and the 18% / 210 CY / five-ponderosa minimum-disturbance targets are current directives or planning premises—not underwriting, metered capacity, takeoffs, or verified feasibility.
- **Medical and hospitality:** the emergency-capable medical concept is not a licensure or clinical-services determination. The spa is a wellness concept, not treatment. Greenhouse dining does not resolve food-service licensing, staffing, insurance, or cost.
- **No audio:** the feature is silent. Chapter titles and on-screen labels are the navigation and explanation layer.
