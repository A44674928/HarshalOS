## Audio editions

- **Narrated edition:** `frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d-narrated.mp4` uses the same picture stream, copied without re-encoding, with the selected AI-generated English narration. Nine chapter-specific voice clips begin at the chapter boundaries above and are padded with silence to the end of each chapter window; the voice track contains no background music.
- **Standalone audio:** `frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d-narration.mp3` is the chapter-aligned 5-minute voice master (160 kb/s, 48 kHz, stereo), measured at −16.5 LUFS integrated and −1.6 dB true peak. The narrated MP4 uses mono AAC-LC at 48 kHz / 48 kb/s with a 4 dB level trim before encoding; the decoded track measures −20.2 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP and keeps the complete film below Wrangler's 25 MiB per-asset limit. The original H.264 picture stream is unchanged; use the separate MP3 for the full-level voice master.

The narration is concept communication, not evidence. It does not establish engineering, approvals, capacities, legal access, clinical readiness, or selected design decisions. The silent master and the separate 9-minute feature are retained unchanged.

## Best-Wishes concepts and their limits

The film treats the Best-Wishes layer as an **aspirational overlay**, not verified engineering, approvals, capacities, routes, or selected design decisions.

- **Water:** seek a reliable potable source, low demand, and approved non-potable reuse where feasible. Source, yield, rights, treatment, storage, routing, capacity, and approvals are TBD.
- **Domestic wastewater:** aspire to a low-water, code-compliant system with a protected reserve. Perc results, treatment method, location, capacity, grades, and approvals are TBD. This is not a lab or clinical waste plan.
- **Power:** explore resilient, lower-carbon options and critical-load priorities. Utility, PV, battery, and backup are candidates only. The 20–50 kW figure is a capability target, not a service-size or compute-load claim.
- **Network:** fiber is preferred if available; an independent backup may be explored. Lab, guest, medical, and controls are shown as separate conceptual zones. Carrier, path, throughput, redundancy, security, and service levels are TBD.
- **Thermal:** high-performance envelope, efficient zoning / heat pumps, balanced ventilation, and possible heat recovery are aspirations. Actual loads, equipment, heat rejection, sizing, and performance are not modeled.
- **Drainage, fire, EMS, and medical:** minimize disturbance, manage runoff/erosion, and pursue responder readiness. Grading, fire-water sizing, legal access, protocols, clinical scope, and agency coordination remain unresolved.
- **Optional tunnel:** a stretch concept only, conditional on geotechnical, water, accessibility, fire/life-safety, egress, permitting, cost, and benefit review.
- **Amenity layer:** sauna / small soak / cold plunge are wellness concepts, not treatment; greenhouse-to-table does not determine food-service licensing, staffing, insurance, operations, or cost.

## Evidence and visual interpretation

- The existing assessor/GIS parcel polygon is a visual frame, **not survey control**. The user's observations about the fence, two-track, apparent slope, street, and road concern are not independently verified, digitized as legal access, or used as a boundary finding. The initial northeast-facing view is a camera-orientation cue only.
- The display surface is still the existing ten-point USGS EPQS inverse-distance interpolation, with the source blockout's 2.5× vertical exaggeration. It is not a gridded design DEM. This film does not substitute `terrain_real/`, which is explicitly synthetic and spatially/elevation-inconsistent.
- The five-tree graphic and 18% / 210 CY values express user-directed retention/minimum-disturbance targets; tree locations, disturbance areas, and earthwork quantities are not surveyed or verified takeoffs.
- Footprints, room bands, amenity markers, circulation, drainage cues, and system diagrams are illustrative. Dashed or animated links are not routes, elevations, pipe/cable sizes, capacity, approval, feasibility, or construction details.
- The site is ray-traced from a schematic blockout; the film uses discrete 3D camera stills with gentle post-render drift and animated interface graphics, not a continuous design-grade digital twin. Rendering and atmospheric effects do not establish material selections, performance, or engineering.
- The user's reported road-rights concern is not independently adjudicated here and is not legal advice.

## Files and reproduction

- `frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d.mp4` — original silent 5-minute picture master.
- `frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d-narrated.mp4` — narrated companion; the H.264 picture stream is copied unchanged and AAC narration is muxed as a separate audio stream.
- `frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d-narration.mp3` — standalone, chapter-aligned voice track.
- `frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d-poster.png` — poster frame shared by the two 5-minute editions.
- `render_frontiership_best_wishes_3d.py` — reproducible silent renderer/compositor. It imports the shared schematic blockout from `../render_outpost_one_pixel_movie.py` and writes its temporary OBJ / still-render cache under `/tmp`.
- `requirements.txt` — pinned CPU-rendering dependencies.

Use Python 3.11 and install the listed requirements, then run:

```bash
python render_frontiership_best_wishes_3d.py --quick   # 640×360 test stills only
python render_frontiership_best_wishes_3d.py           # 1440×810, 32-spp 3D renders + silent 1080p film
```

The renderer uses Mitsuba's `scalar_rgb` CPU variant. Its non-fatal LLVM initialization warning is expected in environments without the optional LLVM runtime; this film uses the working scalar renderer. To reproduce the narrated mux from the included voice track while retaining the silent picture stream:

```bash
ffmpeg -i frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d.mp4 \
  -i frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d-narration.mp3 \
  -map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0 -c:v copy -af asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS,volume=-4dB \
  -c:a aac -b:a 48k -ar 48000 -ac 1 -t 300 -movflags +faststart \
  frontiership-outpost-one-best-wishes-3d-narrated.mp4
```

## Private-preview boundary

The HarshalOS app links both Best-Wishes MP4 editions, the standalone narration MP3, poster, and guide through named `/private/` routes. `scripts/prepare-assets.mjs` copies only these named files and the other specific app runtime assets; it does not publish the terrain package or project folder wholesale. Because the app bundle contains private project and economic assumptions, Cloudflare Access must protect the desktop, direct JS/CSS requests, and every `/private/` and `/terrain/` route. Access is configured outside this repository and is not enforced by the browser app or asset stager; do not deploy to an unprotected staging or preview host.
