## Voice-over transcript

### 01 · Thesis

> Welcome to FRONTIERSHIP OUTPOST ONE, a proposed Safety First high-desert AI lab headquarters. The operating idea brings together long-stay rooms, a frontier-scale AI program, shared amenities, and a small emergency-capable medical concept. The current business hypothesis is a 30-day-or-longer resident stay—never short-term rental—at four thousand eight hundred dollars for each rentable room-month. That is an all-in resident bundle: accommodation, lab and program access, and an amenity package whose final roster is still to be defined. The price is an owner-selected working assumption, not a validated market rate.

### 02 · Room economics

> The current room-count scenario is 62 bedrooms: 57 intended for rental, two for the owners, and three extras left unallocated. Bedrooms are not beds, people, or approved occupancy. Shared occupancy is allowed for planning, but the room price stays at four thousand eight hundred dollars total per month; it is never multiplied by the number of residents. If all 57 rooms were occupied for a full year, gross bundled revenue would be about 3.283 million dollars. That figure is before vacancy, discounts, operating cost, taxes, debt, or service expense. It is full-occupancy arithmetic—not a forecast, profit, or return.

### 03 · Lean repeatable plan

> The selected development approach is one lean, repeatable plan for the entire build, replacing the former cost-unlimited max-house as the active baseline. Standard room modules and consolidated service cores are design tests, not approved drawings. The plan should lower total cost per rentable room while preserving the resident experience. The earlier bermed wing remains on the table only as a test against grading, structure, drainage, energy, egress, and cost. The 18-percent disturbance envelope, 210 cubic yards of grading, and five ponderosa trees retained are project targets, pending survey and measured design.

### 04 · Frontier + safety

> OUTPOST ONE targets 20 to 50 kilowatts of sustained AI capability through rented, bring-your-own compute and a demonstration closet—not a new owned GPU plant or a colocation campus. The program also imagines community commons and a small emergency-capable medical function. It does not yet define clinical services, licensing, staffing, room design, or emergency response. A wellness or greenhouse amenity is not medical treatment. Each included amenity and its operating cost still needs to be defined inside the all-in resident bundle.

### 05 · Proof gates

> This is a concept seeking evidence, not a shovel-ready deal. The discretionary project budget is zero; no investor, grant, sponsor, or customer commitment is secured. Lean construction and operating costs, the financing ask, room schedule, occupancy limits, demand, and contract terms are all still open. Before investment underwriting, validate site access and survey, zoning and use, power, water and wastewater, fire and life safety, medical scope, the four thousand eight hundred dollar bundle and paying demand, and a costed repeatable design. The vision is clear; the numbers and permissions are not yet proven.

## Rendering / provenance notes

- The new cut is graphic-led and intentionally avoids reusing historical on-screen pricing, old owner-share math, approximate room cutaways, or an unverified site layout. It is 1920×1080 H.264 with AAC narration and longer reading holds.
- The current owner scenario is **62 bedrooms** (57 intended rentals, 2 owner bedrooms, 3 unallocated extras). It does not establish beds, bedspaces, residents, legal occupancy, or guaranteed revenue units.
- Full-occupancy gross room revenue is `57 × $4,800 × 12 = $3,283,200/year`. It is shown only as arithmetic, before vacancy and all costs. The legacy 88% occupancy and legacy max-house capex/OPEX/cap-rate assumptions are intentionally omitted.
- The previous 9-minute feature and 5-minute Best-Wishes films are archival and unchanged. The new cut is a separate, current-concept video.
- Render command (requires PyAV and Pillow in the Python environment): `python3 render_outpost_one_investor_cut.py`. Use `--output` for another output path. The project folder is private and is not part of the HarshalOS deployment asset list.
