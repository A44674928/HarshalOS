## Gate detail

### 1. PERC — the boss gate
- Drives everything downstream: septic type (LOS vs conventional), bed ceiling, site footprint, CUP scope.
- **Design basis:** 9,000 gpd (60 beds × 150 gpd) + **100% reserve ≈ 0.7 ac SW** per `FRONTIER_RESIDENCY_MAX_STUDY.md`.
- If perc fails or soil is tight: bed count cuts (60 → 48 → 30 rungs) — re-run pro forma before proceeding. **Kill line: <30 beds buildable = stop, renegotiate price or model.**

### 2. Well yield — the second boss gate
- 72-hour pump test with driller + hydrologist log; test for **6 gpm sustained** (60-bed basis).
- Backup design: 15k-gal cistern + 2nd well abandonment cap already in systems plan — confirm cost before promising 62 beds.
- In parallel with 1 (different vendor, no dependency).

### 3. Geotech — bermed lower level
- Probe the north cut / south walk-out; basalt depth decides bermed 6,300 SF vs cut-and-cover cost.
- Feed result to pro forma capex ($19–24M band) — bermed premium is inside the band; a basalt surprise above it triggers value-engineering review.

### 4. County pre-app + fire district
- **After gate 1** (know the bed count): CUP for lodging >8 rooms, 6-mi road maintenance agreement (who signs), AR-5 50'/35%/WUI Ord 95-1 confirmation.
- Fire district pre-app: defensible space (60'), 30k-gal fire cistern + hydrant, **MED LZ (Ø60' →100' clearing)** notification.
- Exit = written pre-app memo; the road agreement's counterparty (county? neighbors? us) is the make-or-break line item.

### 5. Validate $4,800 (the price gate)
- Three **sponsor-paid** comparables: (a) researcher housing paid by a company, (b) wet-lab bench/seat membership, (c) bundle total (apartment + desk + bench) in SF/Seattle/Boulder — per §7B.
- Pass = proceed at $4,800 · Fail = reprice to what comps support (floor $4,000 → consumption-asset verdict), rerun sensitivity tab, re-present economics.
- Can run any time; must finish **before** capital decision (gate 8).

### 6. Medical counsel + EMS
- Written opinion: stabilize-then-transfer facility stays under AZ licensure thresholds; medical-director + standing-orders agreement (AZ-licensed physician); DEA only if controlled Rx; CLIA only if patient testing ever introduced; RUO research ≠ clinical.
- EMS liaison: county EMS + fire district — LZ registration, transfer protocol (45 min dry / 75 min snow to Flagstaff), ALS jump-kit standards.
- Pass = licensure memo + EMS pre-registration letter on file.

### 7. Brand clearance
- Search FRONTIERSHIP + chosen OUTPOST composite in Cl. 43 (lodging), 35 (community/admin), 41 (events), 42 (software/lab) — USPTO TSSR + state AZ + common-law (the NYC Outpost coliving operator is the watch-item).
- Domains: frontiership.com is held (CA shipbroker) → secure `frontiership.ai` or `frontiership.lab` + property domain.
- **Spend gate:** nothing printed, listed, or advertised under the brand before this clears.

### 8. Capital → ALTA → engineered → permits
- Go/no-go on the full packet (this checklist + pro forma + studies).
- ALTA/NSPS survey → engineered LOS + Coconino C-101 road permit → building permits (state: no state permit beyond county) → construction loan/equity close → NTP.
