## 3. Medical facility (reserved in the build)

**Scope: stabilize-then-transfer, not treatment.** This is what keeps licensure light and the build honest about being 45–75 min from an ER.

| Tier | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **T0 — campus** | AEDs every floor, bleeding-control kits, O₂ on each level, emergency panel (clinic on gen-backed circuit), resident EMT/paramedic stipend, monthly drills | whole house |
| **T1 — core (L1 east, ~3,000 SF)** | 2-bed observation/stabilization bay, treatment room, telemedicine consult room (24/7 physician link), med storage/Rx lockbox, ALS jump kit, decon sink | east wing — **20′ ambulance lane direct from motor court to clinic door** |
| **T2 — air/ground** | **MED LZ Ø60′** light-EMS/UAV pad east-center; upgrade path = clear orchard → 100′ (FAA AC 150/5390-2); pre-registered with county EMS; ground ALS transfer 45 min dry / 75 min snow to Flagstaff (North Country Healthcare) | site |
| **Wet lab (with core)** | BSL-2: Class II BSC, fume hood, autoclave, −80s, cold room, bio-waste contract — **research-use-only (RUO)**: no patient diagnostics = no CLIA trigger | L1 east + LL cold/mech |

**Explicitly NOT included:** surgery, inpatient, birthing, clinical diagnostics — each would trigger heavier AZ licensing. **Confirm with healthcare counsel:** AZ DHS/DOH licensure thresholds for urgent-care/stabilization, medical-director + standing-orders agreement (AZ-licensed physician), DEA only if controlled Rx on site, CLIA only if patient testing ever introduced. Add **EMS liaison + fire-district pre-app** to the gate sequence.

**Why it belongs here:** "Safety First" is the brand *and* the differentiator — remote workers, researchers, and companies pay for a place where a medical event isn't a 75-minute gamble. It's also the one amenity a Flagstaff apartment can never copy.
