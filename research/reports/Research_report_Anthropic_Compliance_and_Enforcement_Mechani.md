# Research Report: Anthropic Compliance, Directives, and Enforcement Mechanisms

This research examines whether and how **Anthropic** enforces compliance with its policies and directives, including documented penalties, access restrictions, financial consequences, and contractual terms across users, commercial partners, employees, and institutional frameworks.

---

## 1. Terms of Service & API Access Enforcement (Users & Developers)

Anthropic imposes multi-tiered enforcement mechanisms for non-compliance with its Consumer Terms of Service, Commercial Terms, and Usage Policy:

* **Account Suspension & Permanent Bans:** Anthropic actively monitors API traffic and web prompt interactions for policy breaches. Common triggers include prompt injection/jailbreak attempts, automated account scraping, misuse of third-party proxy harnesses (e.g., OpenCode, Cline), and frequent IP/proxy rotation.
* **Loss of Subscriptions & Refund Forfeiture:** Under Anthropic's Consumer Terms of Service, if an account is terminated due to a policy violation, the user **forfeits remaining subscription fees and is not entitled to a refund**.
* **API Key & Access Revocation:** On January 27, 2026, Anthropic systematically blocked unauthorized third-party harnesses (such as OpenCode) from routing subscription tokens through unofficial interfaces, enforcing commercial licensing through automated access revocation and account bans.
* **Law Enforcement & Regulatory Reporting:** Anthropic explicitly reserves the right in its Consumer Terms and Privacy Policy to report inputs, outputs, or user data to law enforcement or government authorities under good-faith beliefs of legal non-compliance, fraud, or serious harm.
* **Loss of Copyright Indemnification:** Anthropic's Commercial Terms provide a "Copyright Shield" for enterprise customers, but this protection is explicitly revoked if the customer violates Usage Policy restrictions, alters model outputs, or engages in willful misconduct.

---

## 2. Institutional Compliance & Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP)

Anthropic maintains self-imposed governance commitments through its Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP):

* **Pause Conditions & Tripwires:** Under the RSP, Anthropic sets "AI Safety Level" (ASL) thresholds. If model capabilities reach higher risk tiers (such as bioweapons uplift or automated AI research capabilities) before required safety guardrails are verified, Anthropic commits to pausing model scaling or delaying deployment.
* **Evolution in RSP v3.0 (Feb 2026):** In RSP v3.0, Anthropic replaced strictly rigid pause commitments with adaptive "Risk Reports" and roadmaps, noting that unilateral scaling pauses can be revised if industry competitors do not abide by equivalent safety constraints.

---

## 3. Employee & Internal Compliance

* **Standard Confidentiality & Non-Disparagement:** Anthropic utilizes standard non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and IP assignment terms. 
* **Difference from Competitor Equity Clawbacks:** Unlike OpenAI's past controversial severance agreements (which threatened unvested/vested equity forfeiture for criticizing the company), Anthropic employees who resigned over safety concerns (e.g., high-profile safety researcher resignations in late 2025/2026) have spoken out without reports of equity clawback enforcement.
