## What the reader does with a chapter

The tab under **The Book** lists every chapter with its word count, sets one chapter at
a time, keeps the place you stopped at in the browser, and reports a SHA-256 digest of
the text it loaded. The digest identifies the state of the manuscript — it does not
sign it, authenticate the author, or authorize anything.

> A manuscript is read here, not executed.

**The plaintext is the source.** No build step, no editor, no conversion: a committed
`.txt` file is what the page shows.
