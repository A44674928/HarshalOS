## 🔢 UTS TECHNICAL REFERENCE

### Core Definition

```
Universal Second (US) = Time for light to travel 1 meter in vacuum
                        ≈ 3.33564095 × 10⁻⁹ seconds
                        ≈ 3.34 nanoseconds
```

**Basis:** Speed of light (c = 299,792,458 m/s) - Fundamental physical constant

### Unit Hierarchy

```
1 US  = 1 universal second   ≈ 3.34 nanoseconds
1 UM  = 60 US              ≈ 0.20 microseconds
1 UH  = 60 UM              ≈ 12.02 milliseconds
1 UD  = 24 UH              ≈ 288.60 seconds (4.81 minutes)
1 UY  = 365.25 UD          ≈ 105,525 seconds (29.31 hours)
```

### Universal Date (UD)

- **Definition:** Years since Big Bang in Universal Days
- **Current UD:** ~5.04008475 × 10¹²
- **Universe Age:** ~13.799 billion years
- **Notation:** UD XXXXXXXXXXXX.XXXXXX

### Conversion Factors

| From → To | Factor |
|-----------|--------|
| Earth seconds → US | × 299,792,458 |
| US → Earth seconds | ÷ 299,792,458 |
| 1 Earth second | = 299,792,458 US |
| 1 US | = 3.33564095 × 10⁻⁹ seconds |
