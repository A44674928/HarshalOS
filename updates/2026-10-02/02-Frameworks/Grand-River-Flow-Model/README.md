## 📋 OVERVIEW

The **Grand River Foundation Hyperstructure Flow Model** is a comprehensive three-tiered megastructure framework for the Grand Canyon Hydropower & Submerged Infrastructure Initiative. This gem provides a complete, integration-ready system that enables:

- **Continuous water flow** from the dammed inland sea at -900m (Tier 3) to the surface-level Capitol at +1,200m (Tier 1)
- **Multi-directional human mobility** (vertical, horizontal, lateral) across all tiers
- **Full stakeholder integration** respecting all 360+ identified stakeholders from the Grand Canyon Hydropower Render Spec
- **Sustainable energy distribution** via integrated hydroelectric, nuclear (SMR), and renewable systems

```

    │ Flow: 20,000 m³/s | Pressure: 2-4 MPa | Temp: 8-15°C

    │ Flow: 15,000 m³/s | Pressure: 0.1-0.5 MPa | Temp: 15-25°C
```

**Features:**
- Gravity-fed continuous flow with pump assistance at transitions
- Multi-stage processing: particulate filtering → desalination → purification → quality assurance
- Energy recovery at each tier transition
- Total generation capacity: ~3.5 GW (2.5 GW hydro + 0.7 GW nuclear + 0.3 GW renewable)
