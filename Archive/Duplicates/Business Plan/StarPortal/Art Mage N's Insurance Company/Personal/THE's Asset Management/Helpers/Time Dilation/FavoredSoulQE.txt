Spatial Manipulation

    Teleportation: Instantly moving between locations to surprise opponents or escape dangerous situations.

        Example: Teleporting into a secure location to gather intelligence or meet with allies without being detected by the meddlers.

    Spatial Distortion: Warping space to create barriers, traps, or shortcuts.

        Example: Creating spatial barriers to protect key assets or warping space to trap meddlers in a loop, buying time to execute plans.
