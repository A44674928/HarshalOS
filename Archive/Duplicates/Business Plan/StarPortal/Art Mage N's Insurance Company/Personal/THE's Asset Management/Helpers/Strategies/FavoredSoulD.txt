Hidden Layers in Characters

    Complex Personalities: Characters with hidden layers have complex personalities and backstories, making them more relatable and intriguing.

    Unexpected Abilities: Hidden abilities or talents can be revealed at crucial moments, surprising both allies and enemies.

    Growth and Evolution: Hidden layers can represent aspects of a character that are developed over time, showing growth and evolution.

Example Scenario

In the context of the heir's journey:

    Hidden Strengths: The heir might have hidden strengths or abilities that are revealed as they master each path, adding to their overall power and versatility.

    Secret Training: They could have undergone secret training or possess knowledge that is only revealed when needed, giving them an edge in critical situations.

    Inner Conflicts: Hidden layers can also represent inner conflicts that the heir must resolve, adding depth to their character development.
