Collaboration and Alliances

    Building Alliances: Forming alliances with other powerful entities to create a united front against the meddlers.

        Shared Intelligence: Sharing intelligence and resources with allies to enhance collective security.

        Joint Operations: Conducting joint operations to counter the meddlers' influence.

    Empowering Allies: Strengthening the capabilities of allies to ensure they can also adapt to the meddlers' evolving techniques.

        Training and Support: Providing training and support to allies to enhance their resilience and adaptability.
